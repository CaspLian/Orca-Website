All About Orcas

Fun Facts

  • Social structure: Orcas are highly social animals that live in pods of a few to over 100 members. They have a complex social structure, with some pods led by older females.
  • Name: The name "orca" comes from their Latin species name Orcinus orca, where orcinus translates to mean "Kingdom of the dead"
  • Interactions with humans: Orcas have never been known to attack humans in the wild. They are often very curious about humans and have been known to swim up to boats and say hello to people

About Orcas

The orca, or killer whale, is a toothed whale and the largest member of the oceanic dolphin family. It is the only extant species in the genus Orcinus and is recognizable by its black-and-white patterned body.

Lifespan: 50 - 90 years in the wild

Speed: 35 miles per hour

Tropic Level: Carnivorous

Scientific name: Orcinus Orca

Gestation period: 15 - 18 Months

Length: 20 - 26 ft. (Male), 16 - 23 ft. (Female)
